Luciana Diniz

Born 1970, São Paulo, Brazil  |  Nationalities: Brazilian & Portuguese  |  Based in Europe since 1988  |  luciana.diniz@me.com

PROFILE

Luciana Diniz is a five-time Olympic show jumper (Athens 2004, Beijing 2008 as trainer for Argentina, London 2012, Rio de Janeiro 2016, Tokyo 2020, and Paris 2024 as team reserve) with over 30 years at the top of international equestrian sport, more than 1,800 FEI-registered starts, a 2015 Longines Global Champions Tour title, Rider of the Year title 2012, and a career-high FEI World Ranking of 4th. Her achievements extend beyond competition: she is the founder of an equestrian education brand active in more than 50 countries and 6 continents, and of two technology ventures, the author of two books on elite performance, a public figure with a global following, and the subject of recognition from international sporting and public figures, including a former President of the FEI.

BACKGROUND

Born in São Paulo, Brazil, into a family of athletes of Portuguese descent: her mother, Lica Diniz, is an eight-time Brazilian dressage champion, Pan-American Games bronze medalist, and Brazilian Guinness World Records entrant; her father, Arnaldo dos Santos Diniz, and brothers André and Fábio Diniz were both top 10 national-level polo players.

She began riding at age 9 and launched her competitive career at the Sociedade Hípica Paulista. At 18, she left Brazil for Belgium to train under Nelson Pessoa Filho ("Neco"), one of the sport's leading coaches, which launched her international career.

She has represented Brazil (2004; current flag since 2022, including as Paris 2024 team reserve), Portugal (2006–2022, including three Olympic Games), and Brazil again since July 2022. She holds Brazilian and Portuguese nationality and has been competing in the sport worldwide for more than 30 years.

In 2018 she served as an Athlete Role Model at the Youth Olympic Games.
